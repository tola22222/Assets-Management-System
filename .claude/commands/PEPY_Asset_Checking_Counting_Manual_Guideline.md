# Asset Checking & Counting Manual Guideline

**PEPY Empowering Youth (PEPY)**

> ⚠️ *Draft document*

---

## Objective

To ensure accurate tracking, accountability, and management of all physical assets owned by PEPY through a clear and collaborative checking and verification process.

---

## I. Preparation

### 1. Assign Responsibility
- Asset management, led by the Operations & HR Manager
- The finance team will provide the procurement list to support asset verification during new imports.
- Finance team will schedule for counting assets/inventories with OPM

### 2. Asset Coding
- Operations and HR Manager will generate and assign official asset code numbers for all new assets.
- Asset tags must be affixed immediately upon receipt.

### 3. Updated Asset List
Before any scheduled counting, the Operations and HR Managers will:
- Share and print the latest updated asset list.
- Distribute it to the Finance Manager for reference and coordination.

### 4. Staff Notification
- Notify all departments in advance about the scheduled count.
- Ask assigned users to:
  - Present all physical assets.
  - Report any lost, missing, or damaged items to OPM.

---

## II. Inspection Process

### 1. Inspection
- Conduct the count location by location (office, ED Room, classrooms (ICT, English, YE), meeting rooms, conference room, toilets, storages and school partners (Dream Classes etc.)).
- Match each item with the Registered Assets.

### 2. Tag & ID Verification
- Verify each asset has a PEPY asset code tag.
- Record issues related to untagged or misidentified assets.

### 3. Documentation
- Record each asset's condition and location on a Physical Count in the P-Drive:
  `P:\03- Operations & HR\24- Inventory\01- Fix Assets`

---

## III. Reconciliation & Reporting

### 1. Reconciliation
- Compare physical count results with the existing Asset Register.
- Note discrepancies such as:
  - Missing or relocated items
  - New or untagged items
  - Incorrect descriptions

### 2. Assets Counting
- Counting process will be conducted twice per year, in: **February & August** (separate documents).
- Participants: Accounting and admin officer with OPM.

### 3. Reporting
Submit a Summary Report with:
- Total assets checked
- Action recommendations (repair, disposal, replacement)
- Verified documents signed between Operations & HR Manager and Finance Manager

### 4. Damage and Disposal Reporting
- Operations & HR Manager is responsible for reporting any damaged or unusable assets.
- For disposal requests, Operations and HR Manager must submit a formal report to the Executive Director for review and approval before any item is written off or removed from the Asset Register.

### 5. System Update
Following verification by FM and ED approval, the Asset tracking system must be updated to reflect:
- Accurate status of each asset
- Disposals or damaged items
- New acquisitions and tag assignments

---

## IV. Frequency & Timeline

| Activity | Frequency | Responsible |
|---|---|---|
| Full Asset Count & Reconciliation | February & August | Finance |
| Tagging New Assets | Ongoing | OP&HR |
| Spot Checks | External Auditing Period | OP&HR + Finance + Auditors |

---

## V. Roles & Responsibilities

| Role | Responsibilities |
|---|---|
| **Operations & HR Manager** | Lead the process, coordinate scheduling, maintain records. Assign users, tagging and documentation. |
| **Finance Manager** | Verify on final assets list/report. |
| **Executive Director** | Review and approve final reports and actions. |
